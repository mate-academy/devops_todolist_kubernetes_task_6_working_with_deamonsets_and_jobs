### Deployment

To deploy the background task and scheduled job to the cluster, execute the following commands:
`kubectl apply -f daemonset.yml`
`kubectl apply -f cronjob.yml`

### Validation

To verify that our resources are working correctly, we need to find the specific Pods and Jobs generated by our controllers and check their logs.

1. **Check DaemonSet Logs:**
First, find the exact name of the pod created by the DaemonSet using its label:
`kubectl get pods -n mateapp -l app=mate-server`
Then, substitute `<pod-name>` with the output from the previous command to view the logs:
`kubectl logs <pod-name> -n mateapp`

2. **Check CronJob Logs:**
First, find the completed or running job created by the CronJob:
`kubectl get jobs -n mateapp`
Then, view the logs for that specific job:
`kubectl logs job/<job-name> -n mateapp`
